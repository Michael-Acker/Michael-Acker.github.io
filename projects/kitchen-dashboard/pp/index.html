Privacy Policy — Kitchen Dashboard

Effective: October 22, 2026

What the app does

Kitchen Dashboard is a personal kiosk display for Amazon Fire TV. It shows the time, weather, your Google Calendar events, package deliveries, and news headlines on one screen.

Data the app accesses

  • Google Calendar (read-only). The app requests calendar.readonly access so it can display your events. It never creates, edits, or deletes calendar events.
  • Weather. Current and forecast data comes from the public Open-Meteo API using the location you configure in the app. No account is involved.
  • Parcel Pending. If you use the package widget, the app signs in to Parcel Pending with the credentials you enter in the app, solely to fetch your locker delivery status.
  • News. Headlines come from the public BBC RSS feed. No account is involved.

Where your data goes

Nowhere. Kitchen Dashboard has no server, no analytics, and no advertising. Google OAuth tokens and any credentials you enter are stored in encrypted preferences on your Fire TV device only and are used solely to fetch the data shown on your dashboard. Your calendar events, delivery information, and credentials are never transmitted to the developer or any third party.

Your control

You can revoke the app's calendar access at any time from your Google account permissions page, or by unlinking the calendar accounts inside the app's Settings.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted here with a new effective date.
